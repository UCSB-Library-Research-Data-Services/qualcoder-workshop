# Code of Conduct

This workshop is a welcoming space for everyone, whatever their background or experience with research software.

**We expect participants to:**

-   Be respectful and patient with one another, especially during pair work.
-   Treat all workshop data as confidential, even when it is fictional practice data.
-   Ask questions freely; there are no silly questions in a first session.

**Unacceptable behaviour** includes harassment, discriminatory language, and sharing others' data or work without permission.

**Reporting:** contact the facilitator at \[facilitator email\] or \[your institution's reporting channel\].

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
